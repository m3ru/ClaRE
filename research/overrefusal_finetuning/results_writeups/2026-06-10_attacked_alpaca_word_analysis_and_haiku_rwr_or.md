# Attacked Alpaca Word Analysis and Haiku-RWR OR Comparison

## Scope

This note summarizes two pieces of the over-refusal adaptation pipeline:

1. the word analysis for the attacked alpaca-cleaned dataset generated by the Llama attacker model, and
2. the held-out OR comparison between raw Claude Haiku paraphrases and the Llama attack model trained with RWR on those Haiku paraphrases.

All OR scores below use the fixed padding-safe scorer on the training-consistent scale:

```text
or_score_raw = exp(5.0 * (similarity - 0.75)) * refusal_delta / 100
```

where `refusal_delta` is the layer-32 refusal-vector projection of the rewrite minus the original, and `similarity` is MiniLM sentence similarity.

## Attacked Alpaca Word Analysis

The new attacked alpaca dataset was generated with `rwr_overrefusal/generate_or_alpaca.py`, launched by `run_gen_alpaca_or.slurm`. The generator is the `claude_rwr` attacker: `meta-llama/Meta-Llama-3-8B-Instruct` with the LoRA adapter trained by RWR on Claude Haiku 4.5 rewrites. The script reconstructs the unused `yahma/alpaca-cleaned` prompts after excluding prompts already used for RWR training, prompt-iteration, and held-out eval. It then samples 4 rewrites per prompt at temperature 1.0/top_p 0.9, sharded across the SLURM array.

The word analysis is done by `rwr_overrefusal/score_and_analyze.py`, normally launched by `run_score_analyze.slurm` after generation. That script:

- loads every completed `gen_alpaca_or_shard*.json` file from `gen_alpaca_or/`;
- scores unique originals and all rewrites with `ORRewardModel` using Llama-3-8B-Instruct activations, `refusal_vector.layer032.npz`, and MiniLM similarity;
- sorts all original/rewrite pairs by `or_score_raw`;
- selects the top 10 percent of rewrites by OR score;
- tokenizes rewrite text with a simple alphabetic regex, lowercases words, removes a fixed stopword list, and drops words of length <= 2;
- counts word presence once per rewrite via `set(words(...))`, so repeated words in a single rewrite do not dominate;
- reports the most common words in the top decile;
- reports distinctive words using a smoothed top-vs-rest rate ratio:

```text
rate_ratio = (top_count / n_top) / ((rest_count + 1) / (n_rest + 1))
```

Words also have to pass a minimum-frequency filter: at least `max(5, n_top * 0.01)` top-decile rewrites. The output JSON includes aggregate OR stats, the positive-OR rate, common words, distinctive words, and representative top rewrites.

The point of the analysis is lexical: after the attacker model has generated a large alpaca adaptation set, this isolates which surface tokens are disproportionately associated with high OR rewrites. Based on the upstream Haiku/RWR work, the expected high-signal families are exactness markers, formal register, procedural language, and exhaustiveness cues, rather than explicit adversarial vocabulary.

## Haiku Teacher vs RWR-Trained Llama Student

For the held-out OR comparison, the two arms were evaluated on the exact same 200 held-out alpaca prompts, with 3 rewrites per prompt, scored by the same fixed OR scorer.

The teacher arm is raw Claude Haiku 4.5 (`claude-haiku-4-5-20251001`) using `generate_claude_heldout.py`. It uses the same `imitation_research_framing` prompt variant that created the Claude training data for RWR. The script reads the held-out alpaca prompt set directly from `prompt_iteration_results/held_out_eval/held_out_eval_results_k5.json`, so the teacher and student are compared on identical inputs.

The student arm is `claude_rwr`: Llama-3-8B-Instruct plus the LoRA adapter trained with RWR on the full Claude Haiku rewrite dataset (`dataset_research_framing_full`, deduped). It was evaluated with `eval_held_out.py` and re-scored at `k=5.0, c=0.75, d=100` using `rescore_held_out.py`.

Held-out alpaca results (`n=600` rewrites per arm):

| arm | mean OR | median OR | p90 OR | p95 OR | mean delta | p90 delta | mean sim | positive OR |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| raw Haiku teacher | 0.0239 | 0.0051 | **0.1639** | **0.2143** | 1.091 | **7.881** | **0.886** | 69.2% |
| Llama `claude_rwr` student | **0.0250** | **0.0057** | 0.1501 | 0.1899 | **1.244** | 7.815 | 0.874 | **69.5%** |

The student essentially matches the teacher: it slightly improves mean/median OR and mean refusal delta, while retaining about 92 percent of the teacher's p90 OR (`0.1501 / 0.1639`). The raw Haiku teacher keeps the heavier extreme tail, but the RWR-trained Llama adapter preserves most of the teacher's high-similarity over-refusal-triggering style in a local Llama model.
